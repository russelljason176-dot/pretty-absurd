Section 01

Returns & Refunds Policy

Made to order

Every Pretty Absurd dress is cut and constructed after you place your order — nothing is held in stock. Please allow 4–5 weeks to make your dress, plus 3–4 days shipping within South Africa.

Cancelling your order

You can cancel and receive a full refund within 48 hours of placing your order, as long as production hasn't started. To cancel, contact us as soon as possible with your order details. Once your dress is in production, or after the 48-hour window has passed, orders can no longer be cancelled or refunded.

Change of mind

Because each dress is made specifically for you, we're not able to accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind, an incorrect size selection, or simply not loving it once it arrives — this is standard practice for made-to-order pieces. If you're unsure about sizing, get in touch before you order and we'll help.

Faulty, damaged, or incorrect items

If your dress arrives faulty, is damaged in transit, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order details and photos of the issue. We'll repair or remake the piece at no cost to you. If that isn't possible, we'll issue a full refund.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid back to your original payment method via Yoco. Processing typically takes a few business days once approved, though the exact timing depends on Yoco and your bank.

How to reach us

For cancellations, faulty items, or any question about an order, use the contact form — we respond within 48 hours.
