02PRETTY ABSURD
from Kaylah…

she arrived late, unbothered, and slightly too much for the room… which is, of course, the entire point. this issue is a small love letter to the dress — the one you don't need but will absolutely buy — and to everyone who understands that being pretty is optional, but being absurd is a lifestyle.

we made her by hand, in small numbers, on purpose. what follows is how she came to be… and how you take her home.

Xo Xo, Kaylah
