Kaylah — Designer & Founder
The Story

Clothes that belong in a film you can't quite place.

Women's clothes usually pick a lane: invisible, or trying too hard. Kaylah skipped the choice and built three dresses instead.

Each one shares the same bones — a corset bodice, a sweetheart neckline, a pleated skirt — and tells a different story. Pink satin that feels like a Barbie dream. Ice blue that belongs on a catwalk in Paris. Polka dots that make you think of Marilyn Monroe on a rooftop.

The name says it all. Pretty Absurd is fashion that's deliberately, joyfully out of place. Clothes that make people stare — not because they're strange, but because they're exactly right in all the wrong ways.

The Philosophy
"Pretty Absurd is built on one idea: clothes should make you feel like the most interesting person in the room. Not the most tasteful. The most interesting."

Feminine or serious. Playful or polished. Women hear that split constantly. Kaylah ignored it. The corset boning is serious. The polka dots are playful. Both, at once, on purpose.

The studio — where it begins
The Process

Every seam placed with intention.
The chaos happens when you wear it, not when we make it.

01

Structure first

Every dress starts with the bones. The corset boning defines the silhouette before a single piece of satin is cut.

02

Fabric as character

The fabric is the personality of the piece. Satin catches the light differently. Polka dots change the conversation before you say a word.

03

Details that earn their place

The ruffle at the sweetheart neckline. The lacing at the back. The invisible zip at the side.
