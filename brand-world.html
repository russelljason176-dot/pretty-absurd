Brand World

The Universe of
Pretty Absurd.

Polka dots on subways. Grass in living rooms. A perfectly pressed dress in an entirely wrong place. This is the world the clothes come from.

I.

The dress that doesn't match the setting. The fabric too beautiful for the occasion. The silhouette that stops the room. Nobody plans to remember an outfit. Pretty Absurd makes sure they do.

II.

The references are everywhere: Marilyn Monroe on a subway grate, Barbie in a dream house that isn't quite right, a Vogue editorial shot in a place Vogue would never go. Pretty Absurd borrows from all of it — and puts it in a corset.

III.

This isn't fashion that wants to disappear into the wardrobe. It wants to be worn, looked at, and worn again.
